NOT JUST A PRETTY INTERFACE

Behind the build ✦

A little about the thinking and learning behind this experiment.

01

THE IDEA

I wanted to explore how computer vision could make everyday interactions more intuitive by turning simple hand movements into digital commands.

✳
02

HOW IT WORKS

MediaPipe identifies key points on the hand, while JavaScript interprets finger positions to recognize gestures and control interactive features.

⌘
03

WHAT I LEARNED

Getting the webcam and hand tracking to work together was one of my biggest challenges. This project helped me better understand real-time computer vision, debugging, and designing interactive user experiences.
